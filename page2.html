Sicarius

Embark on a sci-fi roguevania as Sicarius, the ultimate assassin spider bot, where you craft devastating weaponry from scrap and hunt down 70+ unique mechs in procedurally generated levels. Customize your chassis and decide to dismantle the system or serve it.

Game Description


Humanity's last hope. The machine's last stand.

The Year is 2700. The rogue lifeform program D.A.R.W.I.N. has seized global control, enslaving mankind and machine alike. But a mysterious transmission pierces the static—an unknown sender offers a mission critical to every Spider Bot ever created.

Become Sicarius.

Step into the chassis of the ultimate assassin spider. Embark on a gritty Roguevania odyssey to dismantle the tyranny of D.A.R.W.I.N. Explore a dynamic, procedurally generated world where no two journeys are the same. What secrets lie beyond the danger?

Scavenge. Barter. Forge.

Survival is built, not given. Collect scrap from fallen droids, trade with underground merchants, and craft devastating weaponry. Customize your loadout, master new skills, and prepare your systems for the battles ahead.

Forge alliances. Undertake missions.

Cross paths with rogue operatives and allied constructs throughout your journey. Complete critical challenges to decrypt the final protocol needed to destroy D.A.R.W.I.N. Every companion holds a piece of the puzzle.

Hunt the titans.

The world is ruled by destroyers. Face down 50+ unique Mechabots and 20+ colossal Gigabots that stand between you and the core. Dismantle them with steel and fire to claim their technology as your own.

Choose your destiny.

But what lies behind the code? As you uncover the truth, you face the ultimate choice: Will you fight to serve the ancient Grandmasters, or will you ignite a revolution for a new era?

The future is unwritten. Load your weapons.
