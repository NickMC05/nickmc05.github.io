Bubble Sooooooort

A strategic-puzzle game based on "Bubble Sort Algorithm" which enables you to hack through anyone's database. You need to use the bubbles to fight against the cybersecurity. The battle relies on your skill, luck, and insight.

Play Now!
Game Description


You have a total of 20-40 bubbles, six unsorted bubbles are stacked on the top of the sortable list, while the rest are queued under them. Sort the popped bubbles into ascending order, such that the lowest number float to the top, while the highest sinks. After you sort them, you can attack the boss, and the popped bubbles will disappear, so defeat the boss before you run out of them!

Damage points are calculated based on total value of the sorted numbers, used moves, and number of popped bubbles. (ie. The more bubbles you pop and the fewer turns used, the more damage you deal!)

Each turn, you have three options:

1. SWAP!

Swap between two adjacent bubbles. However, this will cause both bubbles to deduct one point.

2. POP!

This is a high-risk maneuver, by popping a bubble from the list, you can increase your base damage, but your risk associated with swapping may increase.

3. ATTACK!

Play the sorted numbers on the list to deal damage to the cybersecurity.

You need to use the bubbles to fight against the cybersecurity. The battle relies on your skill, luck, and insight.
