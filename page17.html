New Obby Master

New Obby Master is a linear-driven obby game in contrast to its predecessor Obby Master, which was a round-based obby mini-game type game. New Obby Master features over 200 purpose-built levels that have to be played in order. Many of the levels/obbies are modified or inspired by maps in Obby Master.
