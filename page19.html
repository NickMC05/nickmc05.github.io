Hydro Detective - Uncovering Water Waste

Hydro Detective - Uncovering Water Waste is a digital, narrative-driven, role playing game that combines elements of exploration, puzzle-solving, and storytelling, challenging players to unravel the secrets of the apartment building and solve the mystery of the high water bill before time runs out. The game is a single player experience for about 10-15 minutes for players age 6 and above.
